## English translation

The road I walk today is not the same as when you walked beside me.
We have gone our separate ways.
I am not the same person now, but I still think of all our memories.
You are still in my dreams. I remember the times we shared.
I think of you whenever the cold wind comes.

I can only lie here with a heavy heart and count the days and hours.
My heart sinks, knowing you will not come back. Tears begin to fall.

Good luck, my love. I hope your new dreams come true.
I will carry on feeling sad, but I will keep our old memories bright.
I hope you find your way through the lonely days.
I send the moon and stars to guide you,
to tell you, even though you may never return.
