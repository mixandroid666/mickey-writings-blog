title: "Solitude"
number: 1
language: en
excerpt: "As I grow older,"
---

As I grow older,

I keep finding that the happiness I love and always seek is a life of solitude.

By solitude,

I do not mean shutting society out completely.

I mean being glad to be alone, somewhere quiet enough to hear my own thoughts speak.

Being around people is not so bad.

But when people voice their opinions, the ego within me grows wild.

It shows its ugly pride, born of ignorance.

Perhaps it is my own ego, swinging its trunk and tusks, always looking for something to defeat.

When the storm settles,

it is I who must sit and mourn the shame it has left behind.

So I would rather live in solitude.

Besides being free from troubling noise,

there is no one to light the fire of my ego and send it dancing until it burns me to ashes.

This may sound as though I blame others too much.

Still, it is the best thought I have for myself at this time.

When I have built enough inner strength and understand enough of how the world works,

perhaps I will turn toward society once more.

Or perhaps it is society itself that is simply too loud?
